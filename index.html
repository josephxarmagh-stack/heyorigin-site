Do I own the website?

You can buy it out any time — one payment and the whole site is yours, files and all. Most owners keep the subscription instead, because hosting, edits, and upkeep stay our problem, not yours.

What happens if I cancel?

You cancel, we stop billing, that’s it. No cancellation fee, no 30-page contract, no hard feelings. If you’re on the website plan, you can buy the site out on the way, or let it go dark.

Is the AI weird with customers?

You tell us — call it: (617) 631-8645. It answers politely, books appointments, takes messages, and hands anything strange straight to you. Your customers just hear a phone that always gets picked up.

Why are you so much cheaper than agencies?

Agencies bill for meetings, account managers, and offices. We build with modern tools, sell three fixed packages instead of custom proposals, and keep zero overhead. You pay for the work, not the theater.

Websites: days, not months — we usually show you a finished draft before you’ve paid anything. The phone receptionist: typically inside a week, trained on your services, prices, and hours.

No. Your number stays exactly the same. Calls you can’t take get answered instead of hitting voicemail. You can start with just nights and weekends if you want.
